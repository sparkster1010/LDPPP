Laughing Dragons Productions Privacy Policy

Company-wide privacy policy for Laughing Dragons Productions apps and digital products.

Effective Date: 6/16/2026

Laughing Dragons Productions (“Laughing Dragons,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) creates and publishes software, mobile apps, games, digital tools, and related services. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information across our products, including local-first apps such as DRI AI and any other programs we offer.

Our core privacy principle is simple: we do not want to store your private data. Whenever our products are designed to run locally, your prompts, conversations, files, settings, and app content stay on your device unless a specific product feature clearly tells you otherwise and you choose to use that feature.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to apps, software, websites, digital products, and services provided by Laughing Dragons Productions. Some products may include additional privacy notices inside the app or on the product page. If a product-specific notice says something different, that product-specific notice controls for that product.

2. Our Local-First Policy

Many Laughing Dragons Productions apps are designed to operate locally on your device. For those products, our policy is to avoid collecting, uploading, or storing your personal content on company servers.

For local-first apps, this means we do not store your prompts, conversations, files, documents, notes, images, generated responses, or other private app content on our servers. Your information stays locally on your device unless you intentionally export it, share it, back it up through your device or operating system, or use an optional online feature that clearly requires internet access.

3. Information We Do Not Collect in Local-First Apps

For local-first apps that do not require an account, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or online processing, we do not collect or store: prompts, chat history, conversations, uploaded files, documents, images, notes, audio, generated responses, personal app content, precise location, contacts, or payment card numbers.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

Some products may save information locally on your device so the app can function properly. This may include app settings, model selections, local chat history, preferences, saved projects, cached files, or other content you create inside the app.

Locally stored information remains on your device and is controlled by you. You can delete local app data through the app settings when available, by uninstalling the app, or through your device’s operating system settings.

5. Optional Online Features

Some current or future products may offer optional online features, such as downloading app updates, downloading local model files, visiting support pages, making purchases through an app store, or contacting us for help. If a feature requires internet access, only the information needed to provide that feature should be used.

We will not use optional online features to collect your private app content unless the product clearly explains the feature and you choose to use it.

6. Accounts and Sign-In

Many Laughing Dragons Productions products do not require an account or sign-in. If a product does not require sign-in, we do not collect account credentials for that product. If we later offer optional accounts for a specific product, that product will explain what information is required and how it is used.

7. Payments and App Store Purchases

If you purchase one of our apps or make an in-app purchase through Google Play, the Apple App Store, or another app marketplace, payment processing is handled by that marketplace. We do not receive or store your full payment card number. The marketplace may provide us with limited purchase status information needed to unlock features or verify purchases.

8. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Our local-first privacy approach is to avoid third-party tracking, behavioral advertising, and analytics that collect personal app content. If a specific product uses analytics, ads, crash reporting, or similar services, that will be disclosed in that product’s store listing, data safety disclosures, or product-specific privacy notice.

9. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We do not sell your prompts, conversations, files, documents, or private app content. We do not share your local app content with third parties for advertising or data brokerage.

We may share limited information only when necessary to operate a requested service, comply with law, prevent fraud or abuse, protect our rights, or complete a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition. For local-first apps that do not collect data, there may be no user data for us to share.

10. Data Retention

For local-first apps, we do not retain your private app content on company servers because we do not collect it. Any local data stored by the app remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

11. Children’s Privacy

Our products are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If a product is directed to children or families, we will provide appropriate disclosures and comply with applicable requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can review and respond.

12. Security

We design our products to reduce the amount of user data we collect. Keeping private app content on your device can reduce exposure to server-side data risks. However, no device, software, or method of storage is perfectly secure. You are responsible for protecting your device with appropriate security settings, passwords, biometrics, and operating system updates.

13. Your Choices

Depending on the product, you may be able to clear local history, disable saved history, delete local files, uninstall the app, decline optional permissions, avoid optional online features, or contact us with privacy questions.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we update it, we will revise the effective date above. Continued use of our products after an update means the updated policy applies.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Laughing Dragons Productions products, contact us at: laughingdragonsproductions@gmail.com
